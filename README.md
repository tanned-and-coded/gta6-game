**Harborline** is an original top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser — no build step, no network, just static files. You are Jett Marlowe, a harbour courier in the sun-soaked city of Port Azura, pulled into smuggling jobs that pay well and attract exactly the wrong attention. Explore a full city of named districts, a harbour and a pier on foot or by car; steal parked cars or pull drivers out of traffic (theft raises your wanted level); follow a four-mission story chain — package runs against the clock, a stolen-car delivery, a dockside drop under police heat, and a final escape — with cash payouts; fight with fists and, once earned, a mouse-aimed pistol; and outrun a wanted-star police pursuit whose patrol cars hunt, intercept and ram you. The HUD shows health, cash, wanted stars and the current objective, and the minimap tracks roads, the harbour, police blips and the mission marker. There is a title screen with New Game / Continue, pause, a game-over screen, and automatic saving to localStorage.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys move and drive, Shift runs, E enters or exits (steals) a car, F punches, the mouse aims and click shoots once Jett is armed, P or Esc pauses, Enter confirms.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts, then open http://localhost:8080
```
